Explain the file or function given in the arguments for interview prep:

1. Read it fully.
2. Give a 3-sentence summary: what it does, where it sits in the pipeline (see `docs/PROJECT.md`), and why it exists.
3. Walk through it block by block. For each block: what it does, tensor shapes where relevant, and the reason for each non-obvious choice.
4. List the 3 lines most likely to come up in an interview and what to say about each.
5. Ask me 3 questions about it, one at a time, and correct my answers. Don't give the answer before I try.
