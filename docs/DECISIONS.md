# Decisions

Every design choice and every experiment change, with the reason. Newest at the bottom. Format: date · decision · why · alternatives considered.

## Starting decisions (2026-11-03)

- **Environment: MuJoCo Pusher-v5** · reads as robotics, contact-rich, 100-step episodes keep rollouts short · alternatives: own maze game (more fun, less robotics), Reacher (too simple, no object).
- **Headline metric: rank correlation of policy rankings** · it is what robotics teams need ("which checkpoint do we deploy?"), mirrors 1X's evaluation result · alternative: pixel error only (doesn't show usefulness).
- **Frames 64x64, 4-frame context** · fits free T4 GPUs, matches DIAMOND's small Atari setup.
- **World model: DIAMOND-style pixel diffusion with EDM** · proven small and playable on one consumer GPU; start from its code, not from scratch.
- **Noise augmentation on context frames, max 0.7** · GameNGen found it essential against drift.
- **State reader instead of true state in the dream** · policy only sees pixels, like a real robot camera; ranking B isolates the reader's error.
- **Policies: SAC via Stable-Baselines3, trained on state, on CPU** · we use RL, we don't research it; saves GPU quota.
- **Eval seeds 10000 to 10049, never in training** · prevents leakage.
- **Compute: free Kaggle GPUs** · no budget; everything kept small.
- **Solo project** (optionally one friend on the policy side only) · every design answer in interviews must be mine.

- **2026-11-04 · Custom fixed camera: distance 1.6, elevation -50°, azimuth 135°, look-at (0.3, -0.3, 0)** (stored as `CAMERA_CONFIG` in `envs/constants.py`) · the default Pusher camera leaves about 70% of the 64x64 frame as black background, and the can is only 2 to 3 pixels; this angle fills the frame with the table and keeps arm, can and goal visible through random episodes · alternatives compared on seed 0 (`outputs/camera_options.png`): straight top-down and 60° tilt (arm leaves the frame at the bottom), side view (arm crowds the left edge).
- **2026-11-04 · All data goes through the `ArmEnv` interface** (`envs/interface.py`: `reset(seed)`, `step(action)`, `frame()`, `state()`), implemented by `PusherEnv` in `envs/pusher.py` · keeps the data source swappable for a phase-2 real arm · alternative: call Gymnasium directly in each script.
- **2026-11-04 · PyTorch port of the faces CNN moved from Step 0 to just before Step 4** · Steps 1 to 3 need no hand-written PyTorch; the port is warm-up for the state reader, which reuses its conv-block design (with flatten instead of global average pooling, since the reader must keep positions) · alternative: do it first as planned (delays MuJoCo work).
- **2026-11-04 · SAC trains through `make_train_env()` in `envs/pusher.py`, which resets every episode from a seed drawn from `TRAIN_SEEDS`** · Stable-Baselines3 needs a standard `gym.Env`, not `ArmEnv`; keeping its construction in `envs/` means `policies/` never calls MuJoCo directly, and the train-seed reset makes "no eval start position seen in training" provable. Scoring and recording still go through `PusherEnv` · alternatives: `gym.make` inside `policies/` (breaks the interface rule), an `ArmEnv`-to-gym adapter (more code for no gain while policies train on true state).
- **2026-11-04 · SAC config: SB3 defaults except `learning_starts` 1,000 (default 100); 300k steps, checkpoint every 20k** · defaults are a known-good baseline for MuJoCo tasks; a few more random steps fill the replay buffer before learning · alternative: tune hyperparameters (not needed: we want a spread of skill, not a record score).
- **2026-11-05 · Second SAC run: 1M steps instead of 300k, everything else identical** (`configs/sac_pusher_1m.yaml`) · `measure_checkpoints` on 20 training seeds showed pushing still improving at 300k (mean final object-goal distance 0.258 m at 20k -> 0.177 m at 200k -> 0.115 ± 0.049 m at 300k; can moved in 100% of episodes from 180k) but the best policy still leaves the can about 11 cm from the goal; the pool needs a top rung that actually reaches it. Fresh run, not resumed: the replay buffer was not saved. Kept on CPU: tiny MLPs and the CPU simulator are the bottleneck, and GPU quota is for Step 4. The 300k run is kept as a record · alternatives: resume from 300k (empty replay buffer, messier), change reward weights (curve was not flat, and that would be a second change). If 1M also flattens far from the goal, reward weights are the next single change.
- **2026-11-06 · Policy pool = option A, 10 brains** (`configs/pool.yaml`): SAC checkpoints 960k, 600k, 400k, 260k, 100k; the 960k brain with Gaussian action noise (std 0.6, 0.8, 1.0, 1.3; noise seeded by start position); a random brain · chosen by screening candidates on the first 50 TRAIN_SEEDS (`policies/pool.py`, never on EVAL_SEEDS): every neighbouring pair is separated by a paired gap > 2 standard errors (1.5 to 2.7 cm), spanning 0.054 to 0.241 m. 960k, not the final checkpoint: the final one was worse and less stable (0.074 ± 0.063 m on 20 seeds). Dropped as near-ties: 20k (= random, neither moves the can), noise 0.3 (= 400k), 140k and 180k (= noise 1.0) · alternative: option B, the same without noise 0.8 (9 brains, 3 noisy copies as in the build guide); A gives a more reliable Spearman and leaves room to hold 2 brains out of the dataset in Step 3.
- **2026-11-06 · Success threshold (pass mark): 3 cm** (`SUCCESS_DISTANCE = 0.03` in `envs/constants.py`) · chosen by eye from `policies/pass_mark_sheet.py` (final frames of 300 training-seed episodes, grouped by final can-to-goal distance): at ~2 cm the can sits centred on the goal, at ~4 cm it has slid toward the rim, so 3 cm marks "clearly on the goal". Chosen before looking at any success rate, never from eval results · alternatives: 5 cm (on the goal but off-centre counts), 7 cm (half on the goal counts). Consequence, accepted: on the eval seeds only sac_960k (30%) and sac_600k (24%) ever succeed, so success rate is a strict secondary number; ranking A and the headline Spearman use mean final distance, which separates all 10 brains.
